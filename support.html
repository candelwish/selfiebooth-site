Support

Selfie Booth 360 turns an iPhone or iPad into a complete event photo booth. Below are answers to common questions.

For anything else, email candelwish@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you within 3 business days.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Selfie Booth, 360 Video, or Panorama.
  2. Allow camera access when prompted (and microphone for video, Photos to save, Local Network for QR sharing).
  3. Tap the shutter to capture. On the review screen, share by AirDrop, show a QR code, or save to Photos.

Opening the admin dashboard

Operator settings are hidden from guests. On the home screen, tap the top-right corner five times, then enter your PIN. The default PIN is 1234 — change it in the dashboard under Security on first use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The app works fully offline. Internet is only needed for the optional Google Drive upload, or for QR sharing (which needs both devices on the same Wi-Fi).

Where are my photos stored?
On the device, organized by event. Use the Gallery or the admin Events screen to find them. They stay on the device until you delete them or remove the app.

The QR code won’t load on a guest’s phone.
Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and that network must allow device-to-device traffic. Many guest or public Wi-Fi networks enable “client isolation,” which blocks this — use AirDrop instead, or save to Photos.

Can I use my own frames and backdrops?
Yes. In the admin dashboard → Frames & Props, import transparent PNGs from Photos or Files. Frames look best at 1536×2048. You are responsible for having the rights to any artwork you import.

The 360 video stops before my set time.
That’s expected — it stops automatically when the rig completes a full rotation. The time setting is a safety cap.

How do I lock the device for unattended use?
Turn on Kiosk mode in the admin dashboard, and for a hard lock also use iOS Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access).

Is this app for kids to use themselves?
No — it’s a tool for event staff to operate at supervised events. Please see our Privacy Policy regarding photos that may include minors.
