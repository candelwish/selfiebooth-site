Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026 · Publisher: Ivan Silva

Summary

Selfie Booth 360 (“the App”) is an event photo booth operated by event staff on their own iPhone or iPad. By default, the App keeps everything on the device and does not transmit your photos, videos, or personal information to us or to any third party. All image processing happens locally on the device. We, the publisher, do not operate any server that receives your data, and we do not collect analytics from the App.

Information the App handles

Photos and videos you create. Media is stored locally on the device that runs the App. We do not receive copies.

Camera and microphone. Used to capture photos and videos, and to optionally record audio during 360 videos. Processed on the device.

Motion data. Used in real time to guide panorama capture and detect a completed 360 rotation. Not stored or transmitted.

Face and gesture detection. To provide AR face props and hands-free capture, the App detects faces and hand gestures on the device using Apple’s on-device frameworks. No biometric template is created, stored, or transmitted, and this detection is not used to identify any individual.

How media can leave the device (only when an operator chooses)

  • AirDrop / system share sheet — sends the selected file directly to a destination the user picks. We are not involved.
  • QR code (local sharing) — the device temporarily serves the selected file over the local Wi-Fi network only, so a guest on the same network can download it. The file is not posted to the internet.
  • Save to Photos — saves to the device’s own photo library, with permission.
  • Optional upload to a Google Drive workflow — only if an administrator configures a destination URL in the App’s settings, the App can send a captured file over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script endpoint that the operator controls. We do not operate, host, or receive data from that endpoint. If no URL is configured, this feature is hidden and no upload occurs.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account or login.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended to be operated by adults (the event organizer) at supervised events, and is not directed to children for their own independent use. Photos taken at an event may include children.

Because the App does not transmit media or personal data to us, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any media created at an event is controlled by the operator (the person or organization running the booth), and its collection, storage, parental-consent, and retention practices are the operator’s responsibility under applicable law (including, in the United States, COPPA). Operators are responsible for obtaining any required parental consent before photographing minors and for how they store or share resulting media.

If you believe a child’s information has been improperly handled, contact the event operator and/or us at candelwish@gmail.com.

Data retention

Media is retained on the device until an operator deletes it in the App or removes the App. We hold no copies because we receive none.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect your data, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our servers. For media held by the operator, direct requests to that operator. Where applicable law (e.g., GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA) grants rights over personal data, you may contact us at candelwish@gmail.com; note our role is limited because we do not hold your media.

Permissions you may be asked for

Camera, Microphone, Photo Library (add-only), Local Network, and Motion. Each is used only for the corresponding feature described above. You can change these in the device’s Settings at any time.

Security

On-device data is protected by iOS’s standard file protections. Optional uploads use HTTPS. The admin PIN is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in plain text.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will revise the effective date above and post the new version at this URL.
