Built for real events

A live, mirror-accurate preview means what guests see is exactly what gets captured. A hidden, PIN-protected admin dashboard lets staff manage events, artwork, and settings without interrupting the line. Kiosk mode locks the interface for unattended use and resets between guests.

Private by design

All photo processing — filters, background replacement, and on-device face and gesture detection — happens on the device. By default, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Sharing is always a deliberate choice made at the booth.

Selfie Booth 360 is a tool for event organizers and staff, intended to be operated by adults at supervised events.
